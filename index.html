About Me

Hello! My name is Ganyuan. You can also call me "Gan".

I am a doctoral researcher at Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris supervised by Prof. Dr. Sébastien Canard and Dr. Qingju Wang.

My research interests include:

  • Provable security with Universal Composability (UC), Game-Based Proofs, with particular interest in Idealized Models e.g., ROM...
  • Secure (multi-party) protocols via compositions of advanced cryptographic primitives (with recent focus on symmetric primitives including oblivious PRF, constrained PRF, and pseudorandom codes (PRC))...

Check out CryptoSearch: a tool I made to make finding citation keys in CryptoBib easier :)

I snowboard 🏂. I practice Aikido 🥋. I do aircraft spotting ✈️. I play Go ⚫⚪.
